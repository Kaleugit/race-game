# Executive Summary Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing `docs/RESUMO-EXECUTIVO.md`.

- The summary is derived from canonical project docs and does not redefine them.
- `docs/EPICOS.md` exists and epic IDs/order match exactly.
- Scope statements are consistent with `docs/PROJECT_SPECS.md`.
- The roadmap stays at macro epic level with no task-level breakdown.
- Milestones and completion signals are observable, not vague slogans.
- Exact dates, budgets, and commitments were not invented.
- Missing facts are called out explicitly as pending decisions or attention points.
- Status is conservative (`EM_REVISAO` unless there is explicit human approval).
- The final document is in the project language.
- The output uses the single canonical path `docs/RESUMO-EXECUTIVO.md`.
